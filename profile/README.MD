### Kea

Kea keeps every account in its own persistent session, so you can stay signed in and switch instantly — without juggling browser profiles, tabs, or repeated logins.

- **Multiple accounts** — keep personal, work, project, and community accounts separate.
- **Instant switching** — move between accounts without reloading or signing in again.
- **Native macOS** — built with Swift, SwiftUI, and WebKit.
- **Private by design** — no backend, no telemetry, no X API.
- **Free & open source** — built in the open for the Mac community.

### Kea helps Kea

Kea is inspired by New Zealand's remarkable alpine parrot.

**30% of voluntary project support helps protect wild kea in New Zealand through Protect the Kea, a project managed by NZ Nature Fund for the Kea Conservation Trust.**
